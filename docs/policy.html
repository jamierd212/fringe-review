Editorial Policy & Corrections

What counts as a review here, what does not, and revisions.

What is counted

A star rating a publication published itself, for a show at the festival in the year listed. Every rating links back to the review it came from, so you can check it. Nothing is written by us and nothing is inferred: if a publication did not award stars, there is no rating to record.

Ratings on other scales

Converted to five stars, labelled as converted, and rounded down. A publication giving 9/10 has said “very good, not perfect”, and rounding that up would put a show on the top line on the strength of a rating nobody gave it. The original is always shown beside the conversion.

One review, once

A publication counts once per show. Where a review carries a byline, two different critics at one publication are two reviews — that is a real second opinion. The same review republished at a new address, or reposted with the headline slightly changed, is one review however many times it appears.

What is not counted

  • Awards, longlists and showcase selections. Real recognition, but not a star rating.
  • Mentions in a round-up that carry no rating of their own.
  • Posts on platforms that refuse to be read. Instagram disallows all crawling, so a rating there cannot be verified by us or by anyone else, and we do not take it on trust.
  • Reviews of another year’s run, or not specific to an Edinburgh festival.

Publications we are still reading

We do not include reviews where every rating we hold from that publication is five stars. A publication we have only ever seen award top marks has told us nothing that discerns one show from another. Nothing is deleted and nothing is judged: the moment such a publication files anything other than a five, everything we hold from it appears.

How the order is decided

Five stars are worth more than four, and low ratings cost: a show cannot climb on volume alone. Shows with no four- or five-star review are listed but not ranked. The average shown is the plain average of every counted rating.

Tickets

Availability comes from the festival’s own listings and is refreshed daily. “No allocation” means the Fringe box office has none left; the venue may still have allocation if contacted directly, which is why that is a choice you make in the filter rather than an assumption we make for you.

Corrections

Something wrong or missing? Write to corrections@fringestars.com.

Please allow 24 hours before writing — most of what arrives is a review we would have collected anyway, a day later. Links help, and a rating is only added once we have seen it on the publication’s own page.

Ratings are collated automatically from publicly available reviews and link back to the publication. Star ratings are the publications’ own; this site only counts them.
