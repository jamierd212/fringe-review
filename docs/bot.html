FringeLeaderboardBot

If you have seen FringeLeaderboardBot/0.1 (+https://fringestars.com/bot.html) in your server logs, this is us.

It collects star ratings for Edinburgh festival shows and collates them into a leaderboard at fringestars.com. Every rating is attributed to the publication that gave it and links straight back to the review on your own site. We publish no review text, no extracts and no images.

How it behaves

  • At most one request per second, and only to pages we have reason to think are reviews.
  • It reads robots.txt and obeys it.
  • It runs once a day, and only during the festival season for most sources.
  • If your server refuses us, we stop. We do not disguise the user-agent or work around a block.

If you would rather we did not

Add this to your robots.txt and we will stop on the next run:

User-agent: FringeLeaderboardBot
Disallow: /

Or email corrections@fringestars.com and we will remove you by hand, along with any ratings of yours already published. No explanation needed.

Corrections

If a rating of yours is recorded wrongly, email corrections@fringestars.com and it will be fixed.
